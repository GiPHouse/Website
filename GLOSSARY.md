# GiPHouse

GiPHouse is a virtual software company of Radboud University Nijmegen, run by students, through which a number of project courses are given. This website supports running those courses.

## Courses and time

**Course**:
One of the university courses given through GiPHouse: SE, SDM or SDE.

**Software Engineering (SE)**:
Bachelor course in the spring semester in which students work as engineers in a project team for a real client.

**System Development Management (SDM)**:
Master course in the spring semester in which students work as managers of a project team for a real client.

**Software Development Entrepreneurship (SDE)**:
Master course in the fall semester in which students in small teams start their own small business around a software product.

**Semester**:
A year and a season (spring or fall); almost everything in GiPHouse belongs to exactly one semester. Spring runs SE and SDM together; fall runs SDE.

## People

**User**:
Anyone who can log in to the website: students, directors and teachers alike.
_Avoid_: Employee

**Student**:
A user who follows one of the courses in a given semester.
_Avoid_: Employee, participant

**Engineer**:
A student following SE, working as a software developer in a project team.

**Manager**:
A student following SDM, managing a project team.

**Entrepreneur**:
A student following SDE.

**Director**:
A student assistant on the Executive Board who helps run GiPHouse (for example as CEO, secretary, facilities, IT, sales, HR or QA). A director is never a student following SDM.
_Avoid_: Teaching assistant, student assistant, SDM student without a project

**Teacher**:
A lecturer responsible for the course material and grading; together the teachers form the Supervisory Board.

## Joining a course

**Application**:
A student's indication that they want to join a course in a given semester, together with the information the teachers need to place them in a team. An application can be rejected, which in practice means the student is not placed in a team.
_Avoid_: Registration, sign-up

**Registration**:
The link between a student and a semester: the course they follow that semester and their team(s). A registration is created when the teachers accept an application, which in practice happens at team assignment. A student has at most one registration per semester, but may register for the same course again in a later year (for example to retake it).
_Avoid_: Application, sign-up

## Teams and projects

**Team**:
A group of students in one semester who work together, with a shared mailing list and repositories. Every registration comes with at least one team, unless the student withdrew.
_Avoid_: Project, group

**Project**:
An assignment from a client, with a description, worked on by one team. Only SE/SDM teams have a project.
_Avoid_: Team

**Client**:
An external organisation, possibly with a contact person, that brings one or more projects to GiPHouse in a spring semester, often returning in later years.
_Avoid_: Customer

## During the semester

**Questionnaire**:
A survey students fill in during a course about the course, their team's progress or their teammates, with a soft and a hard deadline.
_Avoid_: Survey

**Room reservation**:
A booking of a GiPHouse room for a period of time, made by a student with a registration in the current semester, or by a teacher or director.
